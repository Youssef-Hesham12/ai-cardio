# Landing Imagery

Created with the built-in image generation tool. These are illustrative scenes,
not real patients, customers, clinical measurements, or endorsements.

Project assets:

- `public/images/care-conversation.jpg`
- `public/images/cardiac-context.jpg`

The generated PNG originals were converted to JPEG at quality 88 for local web
delivery. All imagery is served locally; no third-party image requests are needed.
The heart and signal fallback PNGs are captures of the actual Three.js scenes,
generated by `scripts/check-landing.mjs`.

## Conversation Prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: wide editorial photograph for a cardiology graduation-project website. A contemporary Egyptian female physician in her late thirties, wearing a white coat over blue scrubs and a stethoscope, listening thoughtfully to an Egyptian male patient in his fifties across a simple clinic desk. Natural, candid conversation, respectful and unposed. Both people clearly visible waist-up, hands naturally resting on the desk, no handshake. Bright real clinic with cool white walls and a small blue cabinet, daylight from a window, true skin texture, clean photographic depth of field without blurring the subjects, refined medical editorial photography. Landscape composition, approximately 3:2, framed with breathing room around both people. Navy and cool white details, natural warm skin tones. No screens, no text, no letters, no branding, no watermarks, no science-fiction elements, no exaggerated smiles. This is an illustrative staged scene, not a real customer or clinical claim.

## Cardiac Context Prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: editorial medical still-life photograph for a cardiology graduation-project website. Close view of a classic navy blue stethoscope resting beside a neatly folded white medical coat and a printed paper ECG strip on a clean cool-white clinic desk. The polished metal chestpiece and the black ink waveform are clearly in focus, real everyday medical objects, restrained refined composition, tactile materials, natural side daylight and soft shadows, no dramatic darkness. Landscape image, approximately 3:2. Objects arranged diagonally with generous space, cool white and muted blue palette with a tiny natural mint accent from a small plant at the distant edge. The ECG is illustrative and contains only waveform strokes with no legible words or numbers. Sophisticated editorial photography, not a product advertisement. No people or hands, no brand names, no text, no watermarks, no futuristic holograms.
