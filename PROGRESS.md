# CardioAssist Prototype

- Public graphite-and-white landing page at `/`, with an animated Three.js cardiac study, functional scene controls, an actual portal preview, and sign-in links.
- Reference-style dark sidebar, header, four summary cards, and two-column dashboard; light theme available.
- Protected doctor routes and persistent demo session.
- Central typed data store: 18 seeded patients, 54 assessments, requests, notifications, reviews, and activity events.
- Patient directory, profiles, editing, assessment forms and drafts, result/explanation charts, doctor reviews, history comparisons, and printable reports.
- Connection requests, simulated mobile submissions, isolated what-if simulator, settings, error controls, and reset.
- Local Inter font, responsive layouts, keyboard-friendly dialogs and mobile navigation.
- Verification: production build, nine portal workflow tests, and five landing-page tests including canvas pixels, motion, responsive framing, reduced motion, and WebGL fallback. Screenshots in test-results.

All scores and explanations are synthetic. No clinical predictions, backend, or real authentication.
